What the UN target means for your closet

“Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse.”UN Sustainable Development Goal 12, Target 12.5

Prevention comes first in that list. For clothes, that means buying less and wearing longer before anything gets recycled. These three habits do exactly that.

Care that makes clothes last

How you wash matters almost as much as what you buy. In the Levi’s life-cycle study, washing and drying at home made up this share of a pair of jeans’ lifetime carbon:5

37%
Wash cold, full loads

Most of a wash’s energy goes into heating water. Cold water is also gentler on dyes and elastic.

Line dry

Skip the dryer. Air drying uses no energy and the heat and tumbling are what wear fibres out.

Wash less

Air out jeans and jumpers between wears and spot-clean marks. Fewer washes means fewer microfibres too.

Fix it early

Re-sew a loose button or a small split the day you spot it. Tiny repairs stop big ones.
